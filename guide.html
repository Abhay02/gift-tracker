Quick start (3 minutes)

  1. Pick a holiday. On first launch, tap the occasion you are shopping for. The colors, icons and countdown change to match it.
  2. Add your people. Go to People and tap Add recipient. Enter a name, a budget for this holiday, and optional notes such as sizes, likes or allergies.
  3. Add gifts. Go to Gifts, tap Add gift, and fill in the gift, price, store, category and delivery method.

Want to explore first? Open Settings → Load sample list to fill the app with example data, then erase it when you are done.

Install it and use it offline

Gift Tracker works in your browser and keeps working with no internet after the first visit. For the best experience, install it like an app:

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome)

Tap the menu (⋮), then Install app or Add to Home screen.

Mac or Windows (Chrome or Edge)

Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open Settings → Install as an app in Gift Tracker.

Use the same browser every time. Your data lives in the browser or installed app you used. Opening the link in a different browser starts a fresh, empty tracker (see Saving and backup below).

One list for every holiday

Each holiday gets its own list with its own date, budget and gifts. Your recipients are shared, so you only enter people once.

  • Switch lists with the menu at the top of the screen.
  • Add a list with the ＋ button and choose another holiday.
  • Plan next year: in Settings → This list, tap Plan next year. It copies the list to next year’s date, keeps budgets, and resets every gift to “not bought”.
  • Budgets are per list. Mom can have $150 for Christmas and $60 for Mother’s Day.

Day-to-day use

Home

Your countdown, total budget, money spent, money left, and progress rings for bought, wrapped and sent. Below that you will see budget vs planned for each person, who is over budget, what is still to buy, and spending by category.

Gifts

Tick Bought, Wrapped and Sent as you go. Ticking Sent also marks the gift Wrapped and Bought. Use the search box and filters to find a gift by person, store or status.

People

A card for each recipient with a progress bar. It turns red when planned gifts go over their budget.

Ideas

Save gift ideas with a price, a link and a star rating before you commit. Tap Move to gifts when you decide to buy.

Shop

Everything not yet bought, grouped by store, so one trip covers it all. Tick items as you buy them, or tap Print list for paper.

Settings

Set the list name, date and total budget. Enter your sales tax % to see an estimated total with tax. Change the currency symbol if needed.

Dark mode: use the sun/moon switch at the top right of the app.

Tips for staying on budget

  • Set each person’s budget before adding gifts, and watch for the red bar.
  • Use Ideas for “maybe” gifts so the budget only counts real choices.
  • Check Home → By category to see where money is going.
  • Add your sales tax % in Settings so totals are realistic.

Saving and backup

Everything saves automatically on your device after every change. Nothing is sent to a server, and no account is needed.

Back up before you…
  • clear your browser data or cookies
  • switch to a new phone or computer
  • open the app in a different browser
Go to Settings → Data and saving → Download backup. To restore, open Settings on the new device and tap Restore from backup.

You can also export any list as a spreadsheet with Export this list (CSV).

FAQ

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After your first visit it is saved on your device and works fully offline.

I opened the link on another device and my data is gone.

Data is stored per device and browser. Download a backup on the old device and restore it on the new one.

Can two people share one list?

Not live. You can send your backup file to a family member to restore on their device.

How do I start over for a new season?

Use Plan next year to copy a list with everything reset, or add a fresh list with ＋.

Can I print my shopping list?

Yes. Open Shop and tap Print list.

Are there updates?

Yes. Updates load automatically the next time you open the app while online. Close and reopen the app once to see them.

The app looks out of date after an update.

Close the app fully and reopen it while online. If it still looks old, reload the page once.
